# Afterglow fictional material set

`demo-afterglow` is a fully fictional inspiration sample. It contains no names, dates, locations, photos, or story details from the preserved personal project.

## Narrative direction

The sample follows an unsent night letter from rain to dawn across six ordinary memories: a cafe meeting, an extra bus stop, a kitchen light, a first trip, small promises, and a daybreak finale.

## Visual prompt set

The seven story images and one dedicated social card were generated with the built-in image generation tool. The shared direction was cinematic photorealistic editorial film photography, anonymous contemporary East Asian atmosphere, deep indigo shadows, warm amber practical light, subtle 35mm grain, restrained melancholy with hope, no clear faces, no logos, and no watermark.

- `instances/demo-afterglow/public/demo-afterglow/cover.webp`: rain-wet night station, empty bench, sealed letter
- `instances/demo-afterglow/public/demo-afterglow/chapter-01.webp`: two cups beside a rainy cafe window
- `instances/demo-afterglow/public/demo-afterglow/chapter-02.webp`: empty late-night bus and two umbrellas
- `instances/demo-afterglow/public/demo-afterglow/chapter-03.webp`: lived-in kitchen with one lamp glowing
- `instances/demo-afterglow/public/demo-afterglow/chapter-04.webp`: notebook, tickets, map, and train window
- `instances/demo-afterglow/public/demo-afterglow/chapter-05.webp`: blank note, plant, and first morning light
- `instances/demo-afterglow/public/demo-afterglow/chapter-06.webp`: curtains, bedside letter, and daybreak
- `instances/demo-afterglow/public/demo-afterglow/social-card.webp`: 1200×630 morning-letter share card with the fictional demo title and privacy-safe subtitle

The generated originals remain outside the repository in the Codex generated-image store. The project copies are the product-consumed deliverables.
